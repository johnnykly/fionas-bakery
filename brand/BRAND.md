# Fiona's Bakery

The identity record for Fiona's Bakery. It should survive any visual redesign.
How it is applied to the website lives in [`../DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md).
Customer-facing copy is always in Swedish. This file is in English so that
designers and agents can work from it; Swedish examples are given verbatim.

## Strategy

### Overview

Fiona's Bakery is a one-person cake studio in Arlöv, just outside Malmö. Fiona is
a trained konditor, sugarpaste decorator and sugar-flower designer. She makes
custom cakes and small bakes to order: wedding cakes with handmade sugar flowers,
children's birthday cakes with modelled figures, christening and baby-shower
cakes, möhippa and party cakes, and trays of cupcakes, cake pops and macarons for
company events and catering.

There is no shop counter. Every order starts with a conversation, and every cake
is made for one occasion.

Before: a family scrolls Instagram for ideas, then gets a generic cake from a
supermarket or a chain konditori. After: they get the cake they pictured, from a
person who listened, made a few kilometres away.

### Audience

Primary:
- **Couples planning a wedding** in Skåne who want sugar flowers and a cake that
  matches the day. They plan months ahead and compare a few makers.
- **Parents planning a child's party, dop or babyshower.** They have a theme in
  mind (an animal, a colour, a character) and want it made real. They plan one to
  three weeks ahead.

Secondary:
- **Friends organising a möhippa, 30-årsfest or 50-årsfest.**
- **Companies and event planners** who need fika, branded cupcakes or a cake for
  an anniversary, kick-off or customer event.

Not for: people looking for the cheapest cake today, or for a café to sit in.

### Positioning

Category: custom celebration cakes, made to order, Malmö area.

Not a café. Not a chain konditori. Not a hobby baker. Not a luxury atelier with
waiting lists and opaque prices.

The local market splits in two. Konditorier sell a fixed range with little
personalisation. High-end cake designers personalise but often feel distant and
expensive. Fiona sits in between: real craft training, open per-slice pricing,
and a personal conversation from first message to delivery.

Structural differentials:
- Trained konditor and sugar-flower designer; the flowers are handmade, petal by
  petal, not plastic or fresh-cut
- Open, per-slice pricing published on the site
- One person from enquiry to delivery
- Delivery in Malmö and nearby; further by agreement

Territory: **the personal cake maker next door who can make anything.**

### Personality

Archetype: the warm craftsperson.

Attributes: Personal, Crafted, Joyful, Generous, Dependable.

What it IS: Warm. Skilled. Straightforward. Happy to help you decide.

What it is NOT: Not precious. Not cutesy. Not corporate. Not a discount bakery.

### References & Anti-References

#### References

- **A good neighbourhood konditori in Skåne:** borrow the warmth, the counter-side
  chat and the pride in classics like prinsesstårta. Do not borrow the dated
  signage or the stock photos of coffee cups.
- **Scandinavian ceramic studios' websites:** borrow the calm layouts, honest
  photos of the made object and plain prices. Do not borrow the cool minimalism
  that makes things feel unaffordable.
- **Fiona's own Instagram:** borrow the closeness: the cake on the table, the
  details up close, the pride in a finished order. Do not borrow the
  uncurated grid.

#### Anti-References

- **Supermarket cake sections:** reject plastic decorations, neon icing and price
  stickers as the main message.
- **Luxury wedding-cake ateliers:** reject "price on request", gold foil
  everywhere and copy that makes ordinary families feel out of place.
- **Generic AI bakery templates:** reject stock photos of croissants, chalkboard
  fonts and "made with love" clichés.

### Promise

Core promise:
- You talk to the person who bakes your cake.
- The cake is made for your occasion, not picked from a shelf.
- You know the price before you order.

Base message: Fiona's Bakery makes custom cakes and small bakes for celebrations in
Malmö, with real craft and a personal conversation from first message to delivery.

Synthesizing phrase: a cake that looks the way the party feels.

### Guardrails

Tone summary: warm, personal, clear, quietly proud.

What the brand cannot be:
- A discount brand
- A luxury brand that hides its prices
- A café or walk-in bakery
- Anything that implies Fiona is a big company

Litmus test: if it could not have been said by Fiona herself, over the phone, to a
parent planning a party, it is wrong.

tårtor till livets fester i Malmö med omnejd. Du pratar med mig hela vägen, från
första idén till att tårtan står på bordet.

Essence: personal craft for the moments people remember.

### Tonal Rules

- Write in Swedish, in the first person singular ("jag"). Fiona is the speaker.
- Use "du", never "ni" as formal plural, except when addressing a couple or a
  company directly.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Name the concrete thing: "hallonmousse och vit choklad", not "fantastiska smaker".
- State prices and lead times plainly.
- No exclamation marks in headings. At most one in a page of body copy.
- No English filler words ("perfect", "amazing") in Swedish copy.
- Never pressure. No countdowns, no "bara 2 platser kvar" unless true.

| We Say | We Never Say |
|---|---|
| "60 kr per bit" | "Pris på förfrågan" |
| "Berätta om festen" | "Fyll i formuläret nedan" |
| "Jag bakar" | "Vi levererar premiumupplevelser" |
| "Handgjorda sockerblommor" | "Exklusiva dekorationer" |
| "Leverans i Malmö med omnejd" | "Vi levererar i hela Sverige" |

## Visual

### Core Colors

These are identity colors, not a design-system palette. The website's applied
roles live in `DESIGN.md`.

- **Terrakotta** `#A9482A` (mandatory). Warmth from the oven; the one accent.
- **Smörgul** `#F3D98B` (mandatory). Butter and vanilla; used for warm fields of
  color, never for text.
- **Kakao** `#2A201B` (mandatory). Text and the logo on light grounds.
- **Mjölvit** `#FBF7F0` (optional). The light ground.
- **Salvia** `#7C8B6F` (optional). Only as a tint in photography styling; never a
  second accent on screen.

Avoid: pastel pink as a brand color (it reads as "kids only" and excludes
weddings and companies), gold foil effects, neon.

### Typefaces

- **Bricolage Grotesque** for headings and the wordmark. Fallback: `ui-sans-serif,
  system-ui, sans-serif`. Licensing: SIL Open Font License 1.1, verified from the
  Fontsource package.
- **Hanken Grotesk** for body text and interface. Fallback: `ui-sans-serif,
  system-ui, sans-serif`. Licensing: SIL Open Font License 1.1, verified from the
  Fontsource package.

### Logo & Marks

- **Wordmark:** "Fiona's Bakery" set in Bricolage Grotesque, tight tracking, in
  Kakao or Mjölvit.
- **Mark:** an arch (a cake dome and a bakery window at once) with a lowercase
  "f" inside, plus a single dot above as a sprinkle or sugar pearl. Used alone as
  the favicon and social avatar.
- Minimum clear space: the height of the arch's dot on all sides.
- Never recolour the mark in more than one color, never add a drop shadow, never
  put it on a busy photo without a solid field behind it.

### Photography & Illustration

Mood: daylight, close, honest, celebratory.

Subjects: the finished cake on a plain ground; sugar-flower details up close; a
slice cut open to show the layers; trays of bakes from above.

Avoid: stock photos, other brands' logos as the hero of a photo, heavy filters,
staged lifestyle scenes with models.

Illustration: none. The cakes are the illustrations.

### Art Direction

Design keywords: Warm ground. Bold, friendly headings. Big real photos. One warm
accent. Rounded but not bubbly. Plenty of air.

Visual territory: **a Scandinavian kitchen table on a Saturday morning.** Light
from the window, a warm wooden surface, butter-yellow and terracotta crockery, and
in the middle, the cake. Everything around it is calm so the cake can be loud.

## Governance

### Claims

Approved (evidence: current site and Fiona's own statement):
- "Utbildad konditor, sockerpastadekoratör och sockerblomsdesigner."
- "Godkänd för F-skatt."
- "60 kr per bit" for cakes; small bakes from 25 kr each.
- "Leverans i Malmö med omnejd; längre bort mot avgift enligt överenskommelse."

Draft, needs Fiona's approval before use:
- Years of experience, number of cakes made, any customer reviews.

Prohibited:
- Testimonials or ratings that are not real and attributable.
- "Ekologiskt", "glutenfritt" or allergy guarantees unless confirmed per order.

### Accessibility Commitments

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA on the website. Every photo of a cake carries a Swedish alt
text describing the cake, not "bild".
